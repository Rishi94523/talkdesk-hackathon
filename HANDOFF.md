## Prompt and behavior contract for the next developer

The following are behavioral requirements, not a claim that every role is connected:

- **Tenant-facing supervisor:** Understand preferences conversationally; search when the caller has asked for properties. Describe one or two matching results using actual returned facts (locality, rent, deposit, size and relevant amenities), state when a showcase is fictional, and proactively offer a viewing, locality research or negotiation. Never read a raw URL aloud, invent missing details or repeatedly add generic caveats. Do not imply that an owner was contacted, a visit was booked, a message was sent, or an offer accepted without a successful recorded result.
- **Search specialist:** Pass locality, BHK and budget, and eventually deposit, amenities and property type; retain full structured result facts and identifiers when handing back to the supervisor. API.SH provides structured listing results; Parallel and Firecrawl contribute separate web leads. Search leads are not automatically listings or owner contact details. Preserve unknown values as unknown. Include the explicitly fictional, locality-adaptive showcase in interactive search and explain filter mismatches.
- **Research specialist:** Investigate relevant local rent/deposit comparisons, amenities, schools, transport, safety and forum experiences with sources. Separate individual reports from established facts; do not fabricate statistics or owner contacts. Summarize actionable context for the negotiation specialist.
- **Visit coordinator:** Identify the selected property and tenant's preferred time, retain property ID and context, resolve the owner's actual contact from a property-specific source or the authorized showcase secret, then request owner outreach. Report pending until the owner explicitly confirms. Record owner response, agreed time and follow-up.
- **Negotiation specialist:** Research comparable prices and local deposit practices, ask for the tenant's target and explicit limits, and provide an owner-liaison agent with property facts, evidence, authorized offer and boundaries. It must not bind the tenant to a contract or invent owner agreement.
- **Owner-liaison voice agent (still to build):** Introduce itself as an AI assistant acting for the prospective tenant, identify the relevant property, discuss the requested visit or authorized negotiation, ask for explicit confirmation and record the outcome. Fetch the specific outreach context using an outreach ID; do not rely on caller ID as tenant authentication. Handle refusal, unanswered call and voicemail separately. The user authorized a demo call to the phone held in `DEMO_OWNER_PHONE`.
- **Memory specialist:** Persist a concise summary at the end of each conversation and structured preference, shown/rejected/saved listing, visit, negotiation and outreach events. Retrieve a bounded summary plus relevant recent facts; avoid repeating rejected or already-shown listings unless requested. Full end-of-call capture and identity isolation still require implementation and testing.

The desired split is functional handoff to a separate owner-call agent for visits and a specialist for negotiation, not an elaborate graph that weakens the conversation.
