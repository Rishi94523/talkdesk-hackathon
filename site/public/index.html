How it works

From "I need a flat" to a signed draft

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Budget, deposit, BHK, area, parking, pets, move-in date. The concierge remembers you next time you call.

  2. 2

    Live search

    Listings come from NoBroker in real time and are ranked against your preferences with a match score.

  3. 3

    Neighbourhood research

    Resident reviews, safety, known rental scams, schools and amenities, gathered from the web in parallel.

  4. 4

    Viewing with the owner

    Our owner-liaison agent phones the owner to confirm a time, or connects you to them directly.

  5. 5

    Negotiation plan

    Market comparison, an opening offer, a target and a walk-away price, plus a script for the conversation.

  6. 6

    Draft agreement

    Agreed terms become a draft 11-month rental agreement for both sides to review, stamp and sign.

Safety first

What we will never do

  • Never take money. We don't collect deposits, token advances or fees.
  • Never invent a listing. Every listing links to its source so you can check it.
  • Never call without consent. Alert calls only start after you agree to them.
  • Never give legal advice. The agreement is a draft for both parties to review.
