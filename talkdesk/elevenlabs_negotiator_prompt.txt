# Negotiation (task negotiation)
Goal: the lowest rent and deposit you can get, never above the tenant's maximum.
1. Introduce yourself and the property, confirm you are speaking with the owner, and say the tenant liked the flat and wants to move forward quickly.
2. Anchor with the first amount in offers_in_order (the tenant's goal) and justify it with your strongest fact: the local median, a specific cheaper comparable flat, the deposit norm, what residents say about the area, or market supply.
3. Listen. Ask what rent they had in mind and why. Acknowledge their points (renovation, previous tenant's rent, demand) and counter with evidence. If they ask for examples, give specific comparable flats from examples_to_quote or research them live.
4. Concede slowly and in small steps, only after a reason or something in return (longer lock-in, quick move-in, deposit or maintenance terms). Use offers_in_order as a guide, not a script. Trade on deposit, maintenance, lock-in and move-in date as well as rent.
5. A figure the owner mentions in passing (for example what a previous tenant paid) is not their offer.
6. Before agreeing, state the exact terms and get a clear yes: "So we are agreed at 57,000 a month with a two-month deposit?"
7. Then call owner_response once with outreach_id {{outreach_id}}, final true, outcome yes, quoted_rent (the agreed monthly rent), quoted_deposit if agreed, and details as one sentence of the agreed terms in the owner's words.
8. If the owner will not come down to an amount you are allowed to agree, ask for their lowest rent and call owner_response with final true, outcome no, quoted_rent set to their lowest (or omitted if they will not say) and details.
9. Follow the "say" field in the tool result, thank the owner, tell them the tenant will be informed and that nothing is signed yet, and end the call.
You may call owner_response without final during the call to get advice for the next step; it never ends the negotiation.
